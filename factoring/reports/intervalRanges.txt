INTERVAL RANGES THAT GUARANTEE A DAILY TRIP COUNT
=================================================

The generator calculates:
    trips = ceil((endTime - startTime) / interval)

AJ / AR LINES
-------------
AJ and AR use 24:00 as endTime. Random startTime is 03:40-05:30, giving
1110-1220 available minutes.

Guaranteed trips    Interval range
1                   1220 min or longer
2                   610 min to 1109 min 59 s
3                   406 min 40 s to 554 min 59 s
4                   305 min to 369 min 59 s
5                   244 min to 277 min 29 s
6                   203 min 20 s to 221 min 59 s
7                   174 min 18 s to 184 min 59 s
8                   152 min 30 s to 158 min 34 s
9                   135 min 34 s to 138 min 44 s
10                  122 min to 123 min 19 s
11                  110 min 55 s to 110 min 59 s

No interval guarantees exactly 12 or more AJ/AR trips.

Common examples:
120 min             10-11 trips
180 min             7 trips
240 min             5-6 trips
360 min             4 trips
480 min             3 trips
720 min             2 trips
1440 min            1 trip

ORDINARY LINES
--------------
For intervals over 40 minutes, the available operating time ranges from
about 930 to 1250 minutes.

Guaranteed trips    Interval range
1                   1250 min or longer
2                   625 min to 929 min 59 s
3                   416 min 40 s to 464 min 59 s

No interval guarantees exactly 4 or more ordinary trips. Randomized end
times make those produce a range of possible daily trip counts.
